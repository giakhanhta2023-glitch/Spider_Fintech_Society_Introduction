# September 2025, management pack

**To:** head of finance · **From:** FP&A · **Date:** 3 October 2025

Revenue beat the plan by 4.4% and EBITDA missed it by 30%. The gap is three
things, two of which reverse.

| | Actual | Budget | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 3,361,050 | 3,220,357 | +140,693 |
| Gross profit | 1,903,569 | 1,929,971 | -26,402 |
| Gross margin | 56.6% | 59.9% | -3.3 points |
| Operating expenses | 1,637,580 | 1,551,580 | -86,000 |
| EBITDA | 265,989 | 378,391 | -112,402 |

**Marketing is 210,000 over plan** because the Q4 brand campaign ran in September
instead of October and November to catch the trading peak. Marketing have
confirmed October and November are now 290,000 below plan, so the full year is
80,000 favourable. No action.

**Gross margin is 3.3 points below plan**, and this one does not reverse.
Transaction fees beat the plan by 151,993, but the split is +313,938 of volume
against -161,945 of rate: volume is 14.3% ahead and the take rate is 0.58%
against a planned 0.62%. Scheme costs scale with volume at a fixed rate, so
selling more at a lower price squeezes the margin from both ends. The pricing
assumption in the plan has not been true since January and the FY2025 forecast
should be rebased on 0.58%.

**Salaries are 124,000 under plan**, of which 96,000 is cost centre CC600, a new
product team that was budgeted and never created. That is not a saving, it is a
plan that describes a company we do not have, and it will read as a favourable
variance every month until the budget is corrected. The other 28,000 is five
support hires that have not been made; volume per support head is up 40% since
January, so this is a service risk rather than an efficiency gain.

## What I would like decided

1. Whether to rebase the FY2025 revenue forecast on a 0.58% take rate, which
   takes about 1.2 million off the full year at current volumes.
2. Who owns closing CC600 in the plan, so the pack stops reporting 96,000 a month
   of savings that are not real.
3. Whether the five support hires are still wanted, given the ticket load.

## About these numbers

Produced from `fpa-actuals.csv` and `fpa-budget.csv` in `september-pack.xlsx`.
The pack ties to the ledger: the check cell on the pack tab reads zero.

Two things about the ledger itself, both reported to the accountant: journal
**JE000261** appears twice, 590,000 of engineering payroll in June, and is counted
once here; and two amounts in the export are stored as text rather than numbers,
which the calculations tab repairs. Neither affects September.

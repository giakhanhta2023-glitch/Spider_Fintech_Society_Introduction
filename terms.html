Terms

FinQuest teaches fintech engineering by building small projects. It is a student society course, run by students, and these are the few rules that come with an account.

last updated September 2026
contact: spiderfintechsociety@gmail.com

This is education, not advice

Everything here teaches how financial software is built. None of it is financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and none of it is a recommendation to buy or sell anything. The tutor is a language model and will sometimes be wrong: check anything that matters.

The data is invented

Every dataset in the course is synthetic, generated by the script in the repository. The prices, transactions, customers and fraud cases are not real, and no real person's financial records appear anywhere in this course.

Your account

Use your own Google account and keep it to yourself. We can suspend an account that is being used to abuse the site, and the most obvious way to do that is hammering the tutor, which costs the society real money per question.

Your work is yours

The notebooks and projects you write while learning belong to you. Put them in your own GitHub account and show them to whoever you like.

Our work is open

The course, the solution keys and the site are published under the MIT licence, copyright the Spider Fintech Society. Fork it, teach from it, change it. The licence text travels with the code in the repository.

No warranty

The site is provided as it is. It is a student project: it may break, it may be offline, and it may be retired. Nothing here is guaranteed to be available, correct, or to keep your progress safe forever. Keep your own code in your own repository and you lose nothing if this site disappears.

Changes

If these terms change, the date at the top changes with them, and the history is in the public repository.

Privacy

FinQuest is a student course. It keeps the least it can: enough to know who you are when you come back, and enough to remember how far you got.

last updated September 2026
contact: spiderfintechsociety@gmail.com

What we store

When you sign in with Google, Google tells us four things and we keep them: your name, your email address, the web address of your profile picture, and the account identifier Google uses for you, which is a number.

As you work through the course we store your progress: experience points, badges, your best drill score and number of attempts per level, which build requirements you have ticked, and a count of how many questions you have asked the tutor.

That is the entire list.

What other members can see

The ranking shows every signed in member your name, the number of levels you have cleared and your experience points. Nothing else: not your email address, not your picture, not what you scored on any particular drill. If you would rather not be named there, ask a committee member and your row will show as a member instead.

What we do not store

No password, because there is never one to store: Google verifies who you are and we only receive the result. No analytics, no advertising, no third party trackers, and no record of which pages you read or how long you spent on them.

Cookies

One cookie, named fq_session. It holds your account number and email, signed so it cannot be edited, and it expires after thirty days. It exists so you stay signed in. There are no other cookies, and none of them are used for advertising.

Who else sees it

  • Google handles the sign in and knows you signed in to this site.
  • Neon hosts the database your account and progress sit in.
  • Vercel hosts the site and, like any web host, processes the requests your browser makes.
  • Anthropic receives what you type to Mou, the tutor, in order to answer it. Those messages are not stored by us. Do not paste anything personal or confidential into the tutor, here or anywhere else.

Nobody buys this data, because it is not for sale, and it is not shared with anyone else.

Deleting it

Email us and we delete your account and your progress. Deleting the account deletes the progress with it, in the same operation. We do not keep a copy afterwards.

How long it is kept

While your account exists. The society runs this course for its members, so if the project is retired the database goes with it.

Children

The course is aimed at university students. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep accounts for them.

Changes

If what we store changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top moves. The page lives in the same public repository as the rest of the site, so its history is readable by anyone.
